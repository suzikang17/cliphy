## Extension Name

Cliphy — YouTube Video Summarizer

## Summary (132 char limit)

Summarize any YouTube video in seconds. Get key takeaways, timestamps, and action items — powered by AI. Queue videos and export.

## Detailed Description

Cliphy turns long YouTube videos into quick, structured summaries so you can skip the fluff and get to the point.

**How it works:**

1. Open any YouTube video
2. Click "Add to Queue" in the side panel
3. Get a full summary in ~30 seconds

**What you get:**

- TLDR — the main point in 1-2 sentences
- Key Takeaways — the important stuff, bulleted
- Timestamps — clickable, jump straight to the moment in the video
- Action Items — what to do next, if applicable

**Features:**

- Queue multiple videos and summarize them back-to-back
- Click any timestamp to jump to that part of the video
- Export as Markdown or plain text — paste into Notion, Docs, or anywhere
- Tag and search your summaries in the Cliphub archive
- Track how much time you've saved

**Free plan:** 5 summaries/month

**Pro plan:** 100 summaries/month + unlimited history + unlimited tags

Cliphy uses AI (Claude by Anthropic) to read the full transcript and produce a structured, useful summary — not just a generic overview.

Privacy: Cliphy only accesses video transcripts on YouTube pages you choose to summarize. No browsing data is collected. See our privacy policy for details.
