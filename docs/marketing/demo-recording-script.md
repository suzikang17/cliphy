# Demo Recording Script

## Setup (before recording)

- Clean browser, no clutter — just YouTube open
- Cliphy already installed & signed in
- Empty queue (or clear it)
- Pick a popular 15-20 min video (something recognizable — a TED talk or tech explainer works well)

## Script (~35 seconds)

### [0-3s] Hook

**Text overlay:** "Summarize any YouTube video in 30 seconds"

**Screen:** On YouTube, video page visible

### [3-8s] Queue it

Click the Cliphy icon → side panel opens → hit "Add to Queue"

The spinner appears on the thumbnail

### [8-12s] Wait (speed up 2x in post)

Brief pause showing the processing state

**Text overlay:** "AI reads the full transcript..."

### [12-20s] Summary lands

Green checkmark appears → click to open summary

Scroll slowly through: TLDR → Key Takeaways → Timestamps

### [20-28s] Interact

Click a timestamp → video jumps to that moment

Quick beat to let it land

### [28-33s] Export

Hit "Copy Markdown" → brief flash of the copied confirmation

### [33-38s] Close

**Text overlay:** "Cliphy — skip the fluff" + store link

Hold for 2 seconds, end

## Production notes

- No voiceover — text overlays + lo-fi music only
- Speed up the processing wait (only dead spot)
- Keep mouse movements smooth and deliberate
- Record at 1080p or higher, crop to focus on the side panel area
- Use a video people will recognize so the summary feels impressive
- Tone: casual & quick, indie dev vibe
- Target: Chrome Web Store listing, landing page, social media
