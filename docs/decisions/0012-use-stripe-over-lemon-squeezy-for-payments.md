# Use Stripe over Lemon Squeezy for payments

**Date:** 2026-02-14 · **Category:** Tech · **Revisit?** no

## Why this choice

Industry standard. Lower fees. Better docs. More control over checkout and subscription management. Customer Portal built-in.

## Options considered

Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Paddle, Polar

## Tradeoffs

More dev work than Lemon Squeezy (which handles tax). We handle sales tax ourselves. More webhook complexity.
