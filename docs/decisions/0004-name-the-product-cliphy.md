# Name the product "Cliphy"

**Date:** 2026-02-14 · **Category:** Product · **Revisit?** no

## Why this choice

Unique and fun to say. 'Clip' ties to video. Works as a verb ("just Cliphy it"). Great brand potential. Hyphy energy.

## Options considered

Cliphy, Cliffy, Klippy, ClipNotes, Klipnotes, QueueTube, VidDigest

## Tradeoffs

Not immediately obvious what it does from the name alone. Need tagline to clarify ("Skip the watch. Get the gist.").
