# Freemium model: 5 free/day, $7/mo Pro

**Date:** 2026-02-14 · **Category:** Pricing · **Revisit?** no

## Why this choice

Free tier drives organic growth and word of mouth, $7 is impulse-subscribe price point, 5/day is enough to hook but not enough for power users

## Options considered

Freemium, free trial only, paid only

## Tradeoffs

Free users cost money (API), need enough Pro conversions to cover
