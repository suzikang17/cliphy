# Price Pro tier at $7/mo ($60/yr)

**Date:** 2026-02-14 · **Category:** Pricing · **Revisit?** yes

## Why this choice

Sweet spot: cheap enough for impulse subscribe, expensive enough to sustain. Undercuts competitors at $10/mo. Annual plan saves ~29% and improves cash flow.

## Options considered

$5/mo, $7/mo, $10/mo, $15/mo

## Tradeoffs

Tight margins at scale if API costs rise. May need to raise price later if usage is heavy. $5/mo would grow faster but less sustainable.
